# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

The Team is an educational resource for anyone 13 and up. We are committed
to providing a safe, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone,
regardless of age, gender, race, religion, nationality, or experience level.

## Our Standards

**Allowed:**
- Respectful questions and feedback
- Reporting errors or broken content
- Constructive suggestions

**NOT Allowed:**
- Harassment, bullying, or discrimination
- Hate speech or offensive language
- Sharing illegal content or tools
- Encouraging hacking without permission
- Spam, advertising, or self-promotion
- Impersonating others
- Sharing personal information of others

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- This repository
- Issues, discussions, and comments
- Any space managed by The Team

## Enforcement

Violations may result in:
1. Warning
2. Temporary ban
3. Permanent ban
4. Reporting to GitHub
